CEO & CTO. Productized Lean Startup methods into software and enterprise programs used inside Google, Salesforce, Intuit, News Corp, and others. Partnered in China through Lean Venture Partners with Chinaccelerator, People Squared, and Google.

2010–2013 Lean Startup Machine 150+ cities · 6 continents

Founded the world’s largest grassroots Lean Startup training program: three-day workshops that taught founders to test the riskiest assumption before writing code.
